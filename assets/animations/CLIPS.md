# Clip manifest

Every animation clip the characters use, with where it comes from and what it is for (GDD §19 M4a.1).
Character space: the model faces +Z; "left/right" are the character's own.

## Conventions

- **Files:** `assets/animations/mixamo/<clip>.fbx`, one clip per file, snake_case, named after what the clip does
  (`hit_react_face`, `knife_stab_straight`), not after the Mixamo download. Prefixes group families:
  `punch_`, `knife_`, `hit_react_`, `getup_`, `jump_`, `crouch_`, `rifle_`.
- **Import:** animation library, humanoid retarget (`mixamorig9_bone_map.tres` for the M1/M2 downloads,
  `mixamorig12_bone_map.tres` for the M4a downloads), post-import script `scripts/tools/mixamo_clip_post_import.gd`.
- **Per-clip data** lives in [`clip_data.json`](clip_data.json) and is applied on import: `trim` (cut to a span),
  `markers` (animation markers), `segments` (extra animations `<clip>_<segment>` in the same library).
  Times are in the source file's time line. Editing it needs a reimport of that clip (Import dock → Reimport;
  headless: delete `.godot/imported/<clip>.fbx-*` and run `godot --headless --import`).
- **Markers** drive timing ([`MeleeCombat`](../../scripts/components/melee_combat.gd)):
  `hit` / `hit_end` = the attack's contact window (active phase), `end` = where the clip stops (the tail is cut),
  `recover` = a reaction gives control back, `down` = the body hits the ground.
  Times below are after trimming (clip time at authored speed).
- **Measure** a clip with `godot --headless --script res://scripts/tools/analyze_clips.gd -- <clip> [--frames]`
  (ground speed, strike contact frames, hip height, air time). Contact = the frame where the striking hand
  reaches furthest out.
- **Placeholders** are generated by `scripts/tools/generate_combat_clips.gd` into `combat_placeholder.tres`
  (library `combat`), with their markers. The AnimationTree is built by `scripts/tools/build_humanoid_tree.gd`.
